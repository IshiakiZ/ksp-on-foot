On Foot @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

Walk the floor of the assembly building and the hangar in first person, at a kerbal's height: among the
ground crew, and up to the craft you are building.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone, the base it stands on, which is a separate download:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
  Copy Keystone's folder (Keystone) into your KSP's GameData folder, and the OnFoot
  folder from this download beside it, and start the game. Without Keystone the game does
  not load this mod. One copy of Keystone serves every mod that uses it. To remove this
  mod, delete the OnFoot folder.

USE
  In the assembly building or the hangar, press Option-W on a Mac (Alt-W elsewhere) to set off on foot.
  Walk with W, A, S and D (the game's own EVA keys), look with the mouse, run with Shift, jump with
  Space. Option-W (Alt-W) or Esc stops, and the game's camera comes back where it was.
  From the space centre, point at the assembly building or the hangar and press Option-W (Alt-W) to
  walk in at its door.
  The Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K / Alt-K) has a page for
  On Foot: speeds, eye height, the mouse, the field of view, a button to set off, and (at the space
  centre) the crew car: an airport baggage tug put on the runway with a kerbal in its seat, on a booster
  (stock rover parts underneath, drawn as the tug; the page can show the stock parts instead). Its top
  speed, 30 km/h unless changed on the page.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11: walking in the assembly building and the crew
  car. Nobody has run it on Linux yet. It has no shaders of its own (the tug is drawn with the game's
  own part shaders) and nothing in it is the Mac's own.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-on-foot
